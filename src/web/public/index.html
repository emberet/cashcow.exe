How it decides

1
Listen everywhere

Independent sources are polled, each on its own schedule. Every one reports what people are talking about.

2
Measure the speed

Not how big a topic is — how fast it's growing. Something already huge is usually too late.

3
Want a second opinion

A spike on one noisy source is ignored. A topic has to show up on at least two independent ones.

4
Screen it out

Real brands, real people, and anything tied to a tragedy are rejected before a single cent is spent.

5
Check who got there first

If other people already made coins for this trend, it skips. Splitting attention forty ways earns nothing.

6
Launch, then exit by rule

It buys a small fixed amount of its own coin and sells on preset rules — never on a hunch.
